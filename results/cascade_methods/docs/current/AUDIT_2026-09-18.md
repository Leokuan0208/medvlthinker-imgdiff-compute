## 1. The judge is MedVLThinker-32B, not Lingshu-32B

`src/labeling/run_judge.py:21`: `--judge_model` defaults to `/data/dan/weights/MedVLThinker-32B-RL_m23k`
and has since the file was created (commit `b61507a`, 2026-06-24). No runner under `runners/` passes the
flag. vLLM's own load line in `logs/sv_sc32_judge_vqamed_open.log` and
`logs/sv_mg_judge_omnimed_open_pool.log` reads `model='/data/dan/weights/MedVLThinker-32B-RL_m23k'`.

The judge is **text-only**: it sees the question, the gold answer and the candidate, never the image
(`run_judge.py:15-19,35-38`). It is a paraphrase-equivalence grader.

Consequences. (i) "Same-family judge (Lingshu-32B judging Lingshu-7B)" — written ≥ 13 times in
`literature/DOMAIN_GUIDE_2026-09-16.md` and in `OPEN_EXPERIMENTS_2026-09-17.md` — is wrong as stated. The
true statement is weaker: judge, Lingshu-7B and Qwen2.5-VL-7B all descend from **Qwen2.5-VL**; MedGemma
does not. (ii) No same-family self-preference signature is measurable: P(judge = 1 | EM = 0) is 0.1389
for Lingshu, 0.1674 for Qwen, 0.1659 for MedGemma (`replication_currency_2026-09-20.json`) — the
generator that shares the judge's base is the *lowest*. (iii) MedEvalKit's own open-ended judge default is
`gpt-4.1-2025-04-14` (`MedEvalKit/utils/utils.py:294`), so the project's judge is not the harness-standard one.

### 2.2 Why the currencies disagree — it is not verbosity (`judge_length_bias_2026-09-20.json`)

- A **pick-the-longest** selector *loses* under the judge (−0.0294 macro); pick-the-shortest −0.0301.
  A logistic regression of the judge label on [EM, token-F1, log words] over 81,554 held-out candidates
  gives a *negative* length coefficient (−0.157). The judge does not reward length.
- On the 72 % of questions (13,220 of 18,452) where the pick is **not longer** than greedy: judge +0.0699, EM **+0.0296** (macro over benchmarks).
  Where the pick is longer: judge +0.1303, EM −0.0648.
- ~32 % of the verifier's upward judge flips have **zero token overlap** with the gold (Kvasir 191/755,
  GEMeX 237/579, OmniMedVQA 272/736). Read by hand (36 sampled): most are genuinely correct against a
  verbose gold — "1." vs "one abnormal finding present", "Esophagogastroduodenoscopy" vs "evidence of upper
  gastrointestinal endoscopy", "Oculus dexter" vs "right eye". A minority are judge errors: "2.5 cm"
  accepted for "11 to 20 millimeters"; and greedy answers marked wrong that mean the same as the accepted
  pick ("Enlarged." rejected, "Enlarged cardiac silhouette" accepted, gold "Stable cardiomegaly").
- **So exact match is an invalid currency where golds are sentences** (oracle@8 strict EM on Kvasir-x1 is
  0.0004), and the judge is mostly right but phrase-sensitive.

### 2.4 Does the probe learn correctness, or distil its judge? A 2×2 (`judge_2x2_2026-09-20.json`)

The 112,770 training rows were re-labelled by MedGemma-27B too (judges agree on 92.85 % of rows; positive
rate 0.1942 vs 0.2388). Two probes, same rows, same recipe (single layer 20, 3 seeds, 4 threads), differing
only in whose labels they were trained on. Macro verifier − greedy:

| | graded by MedVLThinker-32B | graded by MedGemma-27B | lenient EM |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **trained on MedVLThinker-32B labels** | +0.0710 | +0.0658 | +0.0026 |
| **trained on MedGemma-27B labels** | +0.0565 | +0.0804 | +0.0101 |

Each probe scores best under its own training judge. The **off-diagonal (cross-judge) gain is
+0.0565 to +0.0658**; the diagonal is inflated by +0.005 to +0.024 — i.e. **7–30 % of a judge-currency
gain is judge-specific**. This is the project's "paraphrase drift" caveat, now measured at full scale, and
it is the honest number to report: *a probe trained on one LLM judge's labels, graded by an independent
judge, is worth about +0.06 macro.*

**Consensus labels (`judge_consensus_2026-09-20.json`, same recipe).** A probe trained on "both judges say
correct" gains **+0.0703 under MedVLThinker-32B and +0.0733 under MedGemma-27B** (EM +0.0033); trained on
the soft label (A+B)/2: +0.0663 / +0.0748 (EM +0.0057). The *worst case across the two judges* is therefore
+0.0703 for the consensus probe against +0.0658 and +0.0565 for the single-judge probes. The margin
(+0.0045) is at the project's own 0.0033 thread-count floor and rests on 3 seeds, so read it as "consensus
labels cost nothing and are at least as judge-robust", not as a measured win.

### 2.5 Is it templates or an answer prior? (`template_stratified_gain_2026-09-20.json`)

The by-image split keeps images disjoint, but 55–100 % of held-out questions on five benchmarks reuse a
question string seen in the training half (RadImageNet and VQA-Med 100 %, PathVQA 79 %, SLAKE 87 %,
OmniMedVQA 75 %, Kvasir-x1 57 %, GEMeX 24 %, VQA-RAD 8 %). Pooled over benchmarks (micro):

| stratum | n | judge-of-record Δ | MedGemma-27B Δ | lenient EM Δ |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| question string **seen** in train half | 11,630 | +0.1077 [+0.1009, +0.1145] | +0.0956 | +0.0180 |
| question string **novel** | 6,822 | +0.1162 [+0.1071, +0.1255] | +0.1096 | −0.0152 |
| gold answer **seen** in train half | 11,764 | +0.1217 | +0.1040 | +0.0314 |
| gold answer **unseen** | 6,688 | +0.0918 [+0.0824, +0.1010] | +0.0952 | −0.0395 |

Under both judges the gain is as large on novel templates as on repeated ones, and nearly as large where
the gold answer never appeared in training — **the probe is not merely a template or answer prior**.
The exception is PathVQA (repeat +0.0669 vs novel +0.0178; gold-unseen +0.0123). Separately, an
answer-string counter (no image, no hidden state) recovers **25–30 %** of the judge gain
(`stats-baselines.md` Part A row 10; `replication.md` R8) and was never reported for the shipped probe.

### 2.6 What may be claimed today

> Under an LLM judge (MedVLThinker-32B, text-only, sees the gold) an MLP probe on frozen Lingshu-7B
> hidden states, used as a best-of-8 verifier, adds **+0.0737 macro [+0.0608, +0.0864]** over greedy
> decoding on 18,452 held-out questions from eight open-ended medical VQA benchmarks (positive on 6/8;
> benchmark-level interval [+0.0260, +0.1165]). Re-graded on identical picks by an independent judge from a
> different model family (MedGemma-27B-it) the gain is **+0.0679**; a probe graded by a judge it was *not*
> trained on gains **+0.057 to +0.066**. Under string metrics the gain is +0.0156 token-F1 and +0.0047
> lenient exact match (a tie) — string metrics are not valid on the three benchmarks whose golds are
> sentences. The features were extracted in a separate teacher-forced pass at a higher image resolution
> than generation used; "no extra forward pass" is a property of the design, not of what was run.

## 3. Replication: Qwen is solid, MedGemma must be withdrawn (`replication.md`)

**Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct — SOLID.** An independent refit is bitwise identical to
`head_final_stack_qwen_2026-09-13.json` on all eight benchmarks. Judge **+0.0814** [+0.0708, +0.0924],
lenient EM **+0.0276**, strict EM **+0.0187**, token-F1 **+0.0317** — a WIN in every currency
(`replication_currency_2026-09-20.json`). Caveats: under EM only 3/8 benchmarks are individually
significant; Qwen's greedy is weak (PathVQA 0.0715, VQA-Med 0.0133), though benchmarks with greedy < 0.10
contribute only 5.3 % of the macro; after best-of-8 Qwen is still below plain Lingshu greedy on 4/8; and
Lingshu *is* a Qwen2.5-VL-7B fine-tune, so this is a second training recipe of one language model, not a
second family. The Qwen training pool was 132,274 rows across four overlapping caches, 54,835 unique — the
dedup fix was in place before the +0.0820 artifact.

**MedGemma-4b-it — WRONG as a replication.** Verified independently
(`verify_agent_claims` output, §7): every sampled candidate has `gen_tokens = 64` (the cap) on every
benchmark, while MedGemma greedy stops at 4–10 tokens and Lingshu/Qwen samples hit the cap ≤ 0.64 % of the
time. The strings are `"Lungs\n\n\nmodel\nLungs\n\nmodel\n…"` — the sampled arm did not honour
`<end_of_turn>`. 58.5 % of candidates are chat-template garbage, 92.6 % of questions contain one, the
probe's picks average 16.9 words against greedy's 2.6. On questions whose whole pool is clean the judge
gain falls **+0.0436 → +0.0096**; in strict EM the arm is **−0.0751 (0/8 positive)**. Also: the quoted
"+0.0481, 8/8" is the `pooled_singlelayer` arm; the shipped `pooled_ens` arm is **+0.0447, 7/8**.
`src/labeling/run_openvqa.py:180` passes no `stop_token_ids`; why greedy stops and sampling does not was
not pinned down. **Every MedGemma number — TRANSFER_WALL §14, DOMAIN_GUIDE §2.4.4, the 14 Sept deck — is
withdrawn until the pools are regenerated with a 10-question stop-token check first.**

**New (pilot, `pilot_cross_model_transfer_2026-09-18.json`).** The frozen *Lingshu* probes applied zero-shot
to *Qwen's* hidden states give **+0.0451** macro over Qwen greedy — 55 % of the Qwen-native probe's +0.0820
and twice the Qwen-native four-domain probe (+0.0227) — with zero Qwen labels. A label-free ridge map from Qwen's hidden space to
Lingshu's (9,688 unlabelled pairs) raises that to **+0.0603**, 74 % of native
(`pilot_lineage_alignment_2026-09-20.json`).

## 4. "Adds no forward pass" and the resolution mismatch (`code-audit.md` A1–A2)

`src/training_methods/extract_generator_hidden.py:437-439` runs one HuggingFace
`model(**enc, output_hidden_states=True)` per (image, question, candidate), teacher-forced on
`chat_template + answer`. Generation ran under vLLM. Extraction hard-codes `HIGH_PX = 1,003,520`
(`:49`); `run_openvqa.py:52,65` defaults to `cap320` = 250,880 px and **no runner passes `--cap`**. All ten
Lingshu caches record `max_pixels = 1003520`; on PathVQA the saved image-token count is mean 485.4 with
**84.35 % of rows above 320**, the hard ceiling at cap320.

So (a) every reported number comes from states the generator did not produce, and (b) the cost claim
describes a system that has not been run. The honest sentence already exists in the repo and was never
propagated: `VERIFIER_ARCHITECTURES_2026-08-04.md:166-169` — *"free-at-generation is an inference from
the architecture, not a measurement."* Stated as fact in `meetings/shipped_method_2026-09-12.html:203,243`,
`meetings/opentext_progress_2026-09-14.html:355` and `PRIOR_ART_PROBE_VERIFIER_2026-09-14.md:15,42`.
The decided full-resolution regeneration (`OPEN_EXPERIMENTS_2026-09-17.md` §1.0) removes the mismatch;
capturing the states *during* generation is what would make the cost claim true.
