## The question

Our verifier is 24 MLP probes reading FROZEN Lingshu-7B hidden states. Lingshu-7B is itself a
fine-tune of Qwen2.5-VL-7B. If a probe trained on one model in a fine-tuning lineage reads another
member of that lineage without new labels, then verifier onboarding cost drops from
per-benchmark-per-model to per-benchmark-once-per-family. That is the whole practical pitch.

Three arms, in increasing cost, none of which uses a single new label on the target model:

| arm | what it does | what it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **A** zero-shot | feed target states through Lingshu's own standardizer and probes | nothing |
| **B** re-standardised | recompute mean/sd on the target's *unlabelled* states, then the same probes | one forward pass over target prompts |
| **RIDGE** | additionally fit a linear map target-space → Lingshu-space on paired states | running BOTH generators over the same prompts |

A pair is a question where both generators happened to emit the same normalised answer, so both were
teacher-forced on identical text. Pairing rule: same (benchmark, idx, answer up to case/whitespace/trailing punctuation) in both generators' caches, TRAIN halves only, no labels.

## Result: two independent lineage pairs

| arm | pair 1: → Qwen2.5-VL-7B *(the base)* | pair 2: → QoQ-Med-VL-7B *(sibling fine-tune)* |
|---|---:|---:|
| **A** zero-shot | +0.0451 | +0.0426 |
| **B** re-standardised (free) | +0.0490 | +0.0511 |
| **RIDGE**, all pairs | +0.0574 | +0.0564 |
| → ridge over zero-shot | +0.0123 | +0.0138 |
| → **ridge over re-standardise** | +0.0084 | **+0.0053** |
| target-native probe (the ceiling) | +0.0820 | +0.0739 |

All values are macro verifier-minus-greedy over the eight held-out benchmark halves.

**D3's pre-registered kill condition was: the reverse direction is ≈ 0, *or* a second lineage pair
does not reproduce.** The second pair reproduced, and closely — zero-shot within 0.0025 of pair 1, ridge within 0.0010. **D3 is not killed on that clause.**

> **Only one of the two clauses was actually tested.** The reverse direction — fitting a probe on
> Qwen2.5-VL states and reading Lingshu-7B with it, D3(a) — was never run; it needs a frozen Qwen
> probe that does not exist yet.
>
> Get the directions right. The probe is trained on **Lingshu-7B, itself a fine-tune of
> Qwen2.5-VL-7B**. Pair 1 reads **Qwen2.5-VL-7B — the base**, so it is fine-tune → base.
> Pair 2 reads **QoQ-Med-VL-7B, a *sibling* fine-tune of the same base** (qwen2_5_vl, hidden 3584,
> 28 layers, verified from its config), so it is fine-tune → sibling. What is untested is
> base → fine-tune. Two of the three arrows in the family tree are measured; the third is not.

## But the expensive component is the one that does not pay

Read the table by *increment*, not by level. Going from A to B is free — recomputing mean and sd on
the target's own unlabelled states — and it is worth +0.0039 (pair 1) and +0.0085 (pair 2). Going from B to
RIDGE requires running both generators over thousands of shared prompts, and is worth only +0.0084 and +0.0053.

The pair-efficiency sweep shows this is not fixable by collecting fewer pairs:

| pairs fitted | macro | sd over seeds | vs. free re-standardisation |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 100 | +0.0081 | 0.0034 | **worse than doing nothing** |
| 300 | +0.0272 | 0.0034 | **worse than doing nothing** |
| 1,000 | +0.0422 | 0.0022 | **worse than doing nothing** |
| 3,000 | +0.0493 | 0.0038 | +0.0003 |
| 9,688 | +0.0574 | — | +0.0084 |

Below ~3,000 pairs the learned map is **actively harmful** — at 100 pairs it scores
+0.0081 against +0.0451 for doing nothing at all. The map only
reaches parity with free re-standardisation at roughly 3,000 pairs and only clears it at the full
9,688.

*Reproducibility check: this refit of pair 1 at all pairs gives +0.0574 against the
pilot's +0.0603 — deviation -0.00289, within the pre-set tolerance (pass).*

| omnimed_open | 4,461 | 0.4472 | +0.1345 | +0.1352 | +0.1733 |

The gain is concentrated exactly where the probe already had selection skill on its home model —
omnimed, gemex, kvasir — and is ~0 on pathvqa and vqamed. Transfer does not create selection skill;
it carries existing selection skill across the lineage.

## What to claim

**Claim this:** a verifier probe trained on one model's frozen hidden states transfers zero-shot to
other fine-tunes of the same base, and re-standardising on the target's unlabelled states recovers a
little more for free. Replicated on two independent lineage pairs.

**Do not claim this:** that a learned alignment map makes adapting a verifier to a new fine-tune
cheap. It needs thousands of paired generations from both models, is worse than useless below that,
and at full data beats the free baseline by +0.0084 / +0.0053. The ridge map should be
reported as a measured negative, not as the method.
