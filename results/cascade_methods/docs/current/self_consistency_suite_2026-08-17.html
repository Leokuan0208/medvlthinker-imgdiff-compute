Attack 2 · Lingshu-7B · 2026-08-17

Self-consistency across all eight cells

Majority vote over N samples was swept across the whole benchmark suite for the first time. At its best budget it is a macro tie at 2.37× the compute. It wins on two cells, is inert on five, and at N=2 it is a significant loss on four. Two defects turned up on the way — and one of them says the published baseline was already doing this.

Currency divergence · must be stated

The macro at N=8 is a tie under the judge and a win under exact match

Judge +0.0024 [−0.0014, +0.0064] TIE against EM +0.0051 [+0.0017, +0.0088] WIN. Both are positive and the sign agrees on 19 of 24 cell×N pairs, but only one crosses significance. The gap is driven by SLAKE_open (judge +0.0031 vs EM +0.0109) and PMC_VQA (+0.0100 vs +0.0132). Under the project's rule that a judge/EM divergence signals an artefact rather than a gain, the conservative read is the judge's: a tie. Note the direction is the opposite of the known paraphrase-drift trap, where the judge is the generous currency.

The hybrid

Every constraint the vote imposes on the head costs accuracy

The brief's cheap hybrid — let the vote shortlist, let the head break ties — scores 0.4665, which is −0.0350 [−0.0495, −0.0205] worse than simply using the head. Widening the shortlist to the top-two vote counts recovers almost all of it (−0.0047, TIE). The ordering is monotone in how much the vote is allowed to constrain the choice: the vote is not adding information the head lacks, it is removing options the head would have picked correctly. There is no version of the hybrid that beats the head alone.

Independently confirmed on the T=0.4 pools against a real greedy control, where the LoRA verifier beats the vote by +0.0296 (judge) and +0.0192 (EM), and the hybrid again loses to the plain selector.

What actually differs between the slots is the answer-space bias

On PMC-VQA, slot 4 emits letter A on 51.3% of items against a gold marginal of 13.3%, and scores 0.4453; slot 7 emits A on 10.3%, puts 80.4% of its mass on B+C, and scores 0.5813. The correlation between a slot's on-marginal mass and its accuracy is 0.937; the correlation with generated length is 0.344, and mean tokens are flat at 3.018–3.064 across all eight slots. On PATH-VQA-closed the same shape appears as polarity: slot 5 answers "yes" 75.3% of the time against a gold rate of 54.0%, and is the worst slot at 0.7647.

Agreement with the greedy answer is a stable per-slot constant with a 0.20–0.25 spread on the multiple-choice cells. That pattern is consistent with a fixed per-request seed making the n sequences a fixed quantile fan rather than n independent draws — it would explain why the effect is large where the answer is one token from a small skewed space and washes out on multi-token open text. This mechanism is not proven here. Proving it needs an A/B of one fixed seed against per-request seeds, which is a GPU job and was not run.

Consequence · identifiability

On PMC-VQA the N<8 points are not identified — the sign flips

At N=2, a random 2-subset of the fan gives −0.0190 and the first 2 slots of the same fan give +0.0193. A sign flip of 0.038 from nothing but which two samples you get. N=8 uses the whole pool and is invariant across all three tie-break and subset conventions, so it is the only budget at which this cell's number means anything. Every N point in the table above is therefore reported three ways in the artifact; on the exchangeable pools the three agree to ≤0.002.

The reassuring half: the frozen deployed T=0.7 pool — the one every published best-of-8, oracle@8 and coverage number in this project rests on — passes the exchangeability test on all three cells (p = 0.68 / 0.85 / 0.89). The defect is specific to the fixed-seed, short-answer pools generated in the August rounds.

Defect found · the baseline

The published open-cell baseline is already self-consistency@8

Found because vote@8 − greedy came out as exactly 0.0000 with a zero-width bootstrap CI on SLAKE_open — 645 of 645 items identical, which cannot happen between two independently generated arms.

The greedy_ok field in the frozen transfer dumps, which supplies the published “always-7B greedy” values 0.7364 / 0.4650 / 0.3240 for the three open cells — three of the eight macro cells — carries the label of the pool's modal-of-8 answer, not a temperature-0 decode. It matches the modal-of-8 label on 2,339 / 2,345 = 99.74% of items and slot 0 on only 91.68%. The repository already says so in three places, including verifier_n_scaling.py:173 — “NB: this is the MODAL-of-8 answer, i.e. SC@8” — but the label on the headline table does not.

Read the other way round

Deleting self-consistency from the baseline is the cheapest thing this round found

Correcting those three cells to a real greedy decode moves the macro baseline from 0.5971 to 0.6018 — +0.0047 [−0.0007, +0.0102], a tie on the macro but a CI-clean win on PATH_VQA_open — and it cuts the baseline's own compute from 1.51 to 1.00 FLOP-eq, because three of its eight cells were quietly paying for eight samples. Accuracy parity-or-better at −34% compute, with nothing to build.

What is not claimed: no published delta is wrong. Every arm in the open-cell chain was compared against the same greedy_ok field, so the deltas are internally consistent. What is wrong is the label on the baseline and the 1.0 charged for it.

Controls

What was checked before any delta was read

Null tests — all five pass

  • N1 · frozen open metric. Reproduces sel_eff 0.775204, oracle@8 0.626013, greedy 0.449467, n 2345/1468. Max abs deviation 3.6e−07.
  • N2 · multiple-choice grader. Reproduces MedEvalKit's stored correct field row by row on all 33,430 PMC and 2,000 MedXpert rows — zero row disagreements — and the published 0.5427 / 0.2615 cells to 4.4e−05.
  • N3 · closed grader. Zero row disagreements; published 0.8254 / 0.7809 / 0.8409 reproduced to 4.1e−05.
  • N4 · vote machinery. Subset weights sum to 1 at every N; at N=M the exact expectation equals the plain deterministic vote; at N=1 it equals the slot mean; a 1–1 tie returns the earlier slot. Max deviation 0.0.
  • N5 · exchangeability tripwire. vote@2 minus a single random draw, pre-predicted to be ~0. Measured −0.0029 on PMC and ≤0.004 elsewhere — the residual that led to the sampler defect above.

Multiplicity

The 24 cell×N combinations were tested against a permutation null in which the vote's pick is replaced by a uniformly random slot from the same pool. On the per-cell standardised statistic, PMC_VQA@8 sits at z = 7.45 against a null max p95 of 2.52 (p = 0.000) and PATH_VQA_closed@8 at z = 3.96 — both clear the correction. On the raw un-standardised statistic the best observed delta does not clear the grid maximum (p = 0.91), because that version is dominated by the noisiest cell (VQA_RAD_closed, n = 251). Neither correction is needed for the pre-specified endpoints: the macro is a single number, and PMC@8 was published on 2026-08-16 before this grid existed.

Random-gold luck floor on the multiple-choice cells

PMC-VQA's gold is A 13.3 / B 36.9 / C 37.3 / D 12.6 — B+C = 74.2% in this 6,000-item subsample. With gold re-drawn i.i.d. from that marginal, vote@8 scores 0.3058 and oracle@8 scores 0.4927. The measured vote@8 of 0.5507 clears its own floor by +0.2595, so the gain is not letter-marginal luck. MedXpert's gold is near uniform and its floor sits at 0.1988.

Method, in one paragraph

Slots are grouped into equivalence classes by a per-cell normaliser (answer letter on multiple choice; normalised text elsewhere). The winner is the class with the most votes in the subset, ties broken by the smallest slot index present; the returned string is that earliest slot, so a real generated string is always what gets graded — no synthetic string is ever scored. For N < 8 the arm's value is the exact mean over all C(8,N) subsets — 8 at N=1, 28 at N=2, 70 at N=4, 1 at N=8 — not a Monte-Carlo draw, which is strictly stronger than a 3-seed estimate. The three open cells additionally carry three independent generation seeds; the multiple-choice and closed cells rest on a single 8-sample pool per item at N=8, which is stated rather than smoothed over. Every arm returns an answer for every question; an unparseable string is graded wrong, never withheld.

Standing caveats that constrain these numbers
  • The FLOP-eq ratio was measured on the open-text cap320 geometry. Mean prefill varies 275–868 tokens across cells and the ratio was not separately measured on the multiple-choice or closed cells; it is applied uniformly as the best available measurement.
  • The closed cells' sampled pools run at cap320 while the published cells are fullres. Every delta here uses the cap320 greedy control, so the comparison is matched; the published values are shown for orientation only.
  • PMC-VQA is the pre-registered 6,000-item subsample of 33,430 (test_2.csv), the split with zero published verification. An answer-letter-bias audit is still owed on it.
  • The free head has never been scored on a T=0.4 pool — no hidden-state cache exists for those pools. The head-vs-vote comparison therefore runs on the T=0.7 pool where both are scored. Not measured, not estimated.
  • On the frozen T=0.7 pool the "greedy" arm is itself modal-of-8, so the vote@8 vs greedy row on that pool is a tie-break convention difference and not an effect. The claim that voting does not help the open cells rests on the T=0.4 block and the open stage, both of which use a real temperature-0 control.
