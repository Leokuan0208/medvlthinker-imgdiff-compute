## 2. Two corrections to the round's own premise

The round was launched on a strategic brief written the previous turn. **Both of its load-bearing
claims were wrong, and finding that out is the round's most durable product.**

### 2.1 CORRECTION 1 — `selected = oracle@8 × sel_eff` is an EXACT IDENTITY; the additive form is wrong

The brief reasoned with `selected ≈ greedy + sel_eff × (oracle − greedy)`. **That form over-predicts
selected accuracy by +0.090 to +0.111 on every cell.**

Source: `coverage_diagnosis_2026-08-10.json:HEADLINE.1_the_briefs_arithmetic_is_WRONG` (max |err| of the
identity **5.6e-17** over 4 cells); `headroom_percell_2026-08-10.json:null_tests.open_text_bar.identity_check`
(`sel_eff × oracle = 0.485287846`, `selected = 0.485287846`, max abs deviation **0.0**);
`open_diverse_2026-08-10.json:structural_headroom.identity_check` (max abs error **0.0**).

- Measured: oracle@8 **0.626013**, sel_eff **0.775204**, selected **0.485288** (n = 2,345).
- Additive form predicts **0.5863**. Measured is **0.4853**. It fails because it feeds a
  **conditional-mean** sel_eff (0.775 / 0.811) into a **difference-form** formula whose sel_eff is
  **0.2029**.
- **The slope survives; the level does not.** "Coverage has a multiplier" is still true as a marginal
  statement; the projection "oracle 0.626 → 0.70 puts selected at ~0.652" is not. The correct value is
  `0.811 × 0.70 = 0.567`.

**And the marginal multiplier is ~0.45, not 0.81.** Measured conversion of *newly covered* questions
falls monotonically with N (`coverage_diagnosis2_2026-08-10.json:A_multiplier`, deployed ens8_scaled
selector; incumbent in brackets):

| coverage added by | conversion rate | 95% CI | n |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| covered already at N=1 | 0.9345 [0.9013] | — | 993 |
| sample 2 | 0.6708 [0.6522] | [0.5963, 0.7453] | 161 |
| samples 3–4 | 0.5309 [0.4877] | [0.4506, 0.6049] | 162 |
| samples 5–8 | **0.4474** [0.3882] | **[0.3684, 0.5263]** | 152 |

Realised `d(selected)/d(oracle)`: **0.4147** over N=1→8, **0.3026** over N=4→8, **0.400** at N=7→8
(deployed). **The brief's 0.81 is optimistic by ~1.8× and the CI on the marginal rate excludes it.**

### 2.2 CORRECTION 2 — "coverage has the headroom, selection is exhausted" is BACKWARDS

Free upper bounds on the 8-cell macro, both charging no extra generation cost
(`coverage_diagnosis_2026-08-10.json:HEADLINE.5_what_this_means_for_the_round` and the numeric block
`macro8_if_open_arm_reached_its_iid_sampling_CEILING` / `..._PERFECT_selector_over_the_CURRENT_8_pool`):

| ceiling | macro | vs always-32B-direct |
|---|---:|---:|
| open arm at its **iid sampling ceiling** (N=∞, capture–recapture) | 0.66585 | **+0.00915** |
| open arm with a **PERFECT selector over the CURRENT 8-pool** | 0.68677 | **+0.03007** |

**The selection ceiling is 3.3× the coverage ceiling.** ~20 approaches converging at sel_eff 0.80–0.81
means selection is **hard**, not that it is **small**. Two supporting facts from the same artifact:

- **It is a CAPABILITY failure, not a DIVERSITY failure.** The no-coverage subset is the *high*-diversity
  subset: mean distinct answers **5.17 vs 3.00**, modal share **0.432 vs 0.701**, normalized entropy
  **0.830 vs 0.522**; 50.2% of no-coverage questions already emit 6–8 distinct answers out of 8, and only
  7.8% emit one answer eight times. oracle@8 by exploration stratum: **0.890 at n_distinct=1 → 0.364 at
  6–8**. An **independent 16-sample redraw (3× the budget) rescues only 21.2%** of them.
- **They are not near misses, so image-side interventions are not indicated.** Best token-F1 of any
  pooled answer against gold on the no-coverage subset is **0.113** (vs 0.889 on recoverable); **71.5%
  have ZERO gold tokens anywhere in the 8-answer pool**; only 10.1% reach F1 ≥ 0.5.
- **The iid sampling ceiling is below the bar on 2 of 3 open cells.** Capture–recapture reachable share
  slake **0.917** / vqa_rad **0.692** / pathvqa **0.626**; at the measured multiplier the oracle needed
  merely to TIE always-32B-direct is **0.958 / 0.868 / 0.518**. SLAKE-open and VQA-RAD-open are
  **unreachable by iid sampling at any N**.

**A third correction rides along:** the brief's open-text arithmetic was **sample-weighted** while the
target is **macro**. On macro, 32B-direct over the three open cells is **0.5982** (not 0.5168) and
oracle@8 is **0.6752** (not 0.6260) — so the incumbent open arm sits **0.0647 BELOW** direct on the open
half, a much bigger hole than the sample-weighted view suggested
(`coverage_diagnosis_2026-08-10.json:part1_oracle_at_N.macro` vs `.sample_weighted`).

## 3. The structural fact the round established: the ceiling is not the problem, identifiability is

p10 = P(7B right ∧ 32B wrong)** — that is an identity, not a bound with slack: the item-level 2-way
oracle equals `1 − P(both wrong)` and its gain over direct equals p10 exactly
(`headroom_percell_2026-08-10.json:per_cell.*.ceilings.item_oracle_7B_or_32Bdirect`).

  (the per-cell winner is chosen on the eval itself).

## 10. What the accumulated evidence now implies

**Can this architecture beat always-32B-direct on macro? On the evidence now in hand, not by any
route this project has been able to find — and the reason is now diagnosed rather than merely observed.**

The argument, in four measured steps:

1. **The headroom is enormous and none of it is reachable.** Σ p10 = 0.529 ⇒ **+0.0661 macro** if a
   router perfectly identified every question the 7B gets right and the 32B gets wrong. Realised:
   **+0.00083 — a 1.3% conversion.** Every cheap signal sits at AUROC 0.57–0.64 among disagreements, and
   on the single highest-headroom cell (MedXpert, p10 = 0.116) it is **0.4877 — chance**. This is the
   **recoverability wall** in its sharpest form yet: sixteen mechanisms have now hit it.
2. **The method degenerates to the baseline on half the macro weight.** Four of five MCQ cells run
   *exactly* always-32B-direct; the certified veto certifies zero items there and extending it is worth
   **+0.0000**. A method that *is* the baseline on 50% of the weight can only win from the other 50%,
   and it needs +0.0236 summed from cells whose individual p10 caps are 0.036–0.116 at ~1% conversion.
3. **Nothing already measured can be rearranged into a win.** The eval-visible best-per-cell
   reassignment of all shipped operating points reaches **+0.00277** against a **+0.0029** bar — and
   that number is already cheating. There is no free lunch left in the existing measurements.
4. **The two obvious new levers are now measured and both are too small.** Coverage: the iid sampling
   ceiling is **+0.0091** macro and it is **unreachable** on 2 of 3 open cells at any N, because the
   uncovered questions are high-diversity capability failures with **zero gold tokens in the pool 71.5%
   of the time**, not exploration failures. Selection: the perfect-selector ceiling is **+0.0301**, but
   ~20 architectures converge at sel_eff 0.80–0.81 with a **seed spread (~0.021) that exceeds every
   architectural effect**. And on top of both, **the open-text measurement floor is ±0.008 per cell —
   which is 1.03× the +0.00773 gain each open cell would have to contribute to clear the bar**
   (`open_diverse_2026-08-10.json:macro_translation.all_three_open_cells.*.per_cell_gain_needed`).
   In macro terms the same floor is **±0.00183 = 63% of the +0.0029 significance bar** and **2.3× the
   shipped +0.0008 delta**. *(Corrected 2026-08-12 — this line previously read "4.4× the entire delta
   being chased", which divided a per-cell figure by a macro figure. See §9.8.)*

**The honest reading: the tie is not a near miss, it is a fixed point.** The architecture's win
condition requires converting a per-question identifiability signal that four years of this project's
worth of mechanisms have shown does not exist at the needed strength — and the remaining ceilings, even
if perfectly harvested, are the same order as the noise floor of the measurement itself.

> deployed selector, **+0.0615 [+0.0514, +0.0715] WIN** with the frozen 8-seed selector, at

**Together those are a paper: "a format-aware adaptive cascade delivers a reasoning-32B's accuracy
budget at a tenth of its latency and energy, and holds parity with a direct 32B while spending less
than the previously shipped configuration."** That is defensible, fully measured, and does not require
the vs-direct accuracy win that four attacks have now failed to produce. **It should be stated with its
limits attached:** parity, not superiority, against always-32B-direct; the compute saving is real but
**concentrated on low-escalation cells**, not uniform; and every cheap tie-preserving policy reaches its
cost by routing two of the three open cells straight to the 32B.

exact-match currency, n=795) — **while noting it is worth only ~+0.0016 on the 8-cell macro**;
