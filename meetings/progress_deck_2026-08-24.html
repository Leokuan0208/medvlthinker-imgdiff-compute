Where we were on 17 August

Three datasets could not carry a paper

The 17 Aug deck ended on a working head and an open question: the open-text arm had PathVQA-open (1,500), SLAKE-open (645) and VQA-RAD-open (200) — 2,345 questions total, two of them small enough that a single bad batch moves the number. The instruction was to go fully open-text and get more medical datasets.

The ask

More open-text medical data, Lingshu-32B as the baseline, and make the MLP head as good as it can be.

What that required

Building cells from scratch: licence agreements, image pulls, de-duplication, generation, 32B judging and hidden-state extraction for every one.

What it produced

Four new cells and 32 feature caches (8 cells × 4 sampling temperatures), plus the negative results that bound what the head can do.

GEMeX is the most out-of-distribution cell we have — the largest k-NN distance to the head’s training rows (7.228, above OmniMedVQA’s 6.967) and the lowest domain-classifier confidence (0.693) — and the head wins on it anyway. Distance from the training distribution does not predict whether selection helps, which is why the method cannot simply be gated on how familiar a dataset looks.

The decomposition predicted this: temperature acts directly on the penalty term, so the cells whose penalty exceeded their skill should recover when the pool is cooled. VQA-Med is monotone in T (+0.0011 → −0.0379) and VQA-RAD flips from −0.0250 to +0.0050. Kvasir-x1 goes the other way, which is consistent — its penalty was already small and it wants the diversity.
The honest caveat. Tuning T per cell is worth +0.0117 mean head − greedy, but that number picks the temperature using the eval labels. Best-T is 0.7 for only 1 of 8 cells, so a single temperature chosen out of sample is not 0.7 either. A deployable version needs a rule that picks T without looking — that experiment is running now.

Negative results · the bulk of the week

Eight ways of improving the head that did not work

Architecture — 7 variants

Domain breadth

Budget-matched at 20k rows so breadth is not confounded with volume, the mean slope is +0.00868 [−0.00143, +0.01997] once the duplicated Kvasir source is collapsed. Interval includes zero.

Bigger pools

Uniting T=0.2/0.4/0.7/1.0 raises coverage and makes the head worse on 6 of 7 cells against its own best single temperature. Coverage is not the binding constraint.

Smaller pools

The converse also fails. Five label-free pruners at k∈{2,3,4,6}; the best fixed rule is worth −0.0000. Pool size is not what limits selection.

Self-consistency as a scorer

Flat at the random-pick floor and does not improve with N: 0.0933 at N=2, 0.0800 at N=16, while oracle climbs 0.156 → 0.418. More samples cannot rescue it.

Greedy-anchored veto

The greedy answer is already in the pool 67–99% of the time and the head overrides it. Keeping it unless the head clears a margin is worth +0.0007 — and at most +0.0022 even with the margin fitted in-sample.

Detecting when to trust it

Six detectors; after GEMeX arrived, none order the cells. The router built on the best one is worse than always selecting, because it sends 99% of GEMeX to greedy and throws away the win.

Answer kind

Tested within cells so the cell is held fixed. Longer answers favour the head in 6/8 cells but the mean is +0.0054 and two cells go firmly the other way.

Next step · running now

What we do next

Temperature-matched head

The head is fitted on T=0.7 pools but the best deployment temperature is 0.2 or 0.4 on five cells — so on most of them it scores a distribution it was never trained on. Cold training pools are generating now for all four training domains. This is the one explanation of the cold-pool gain that needs no eval labels.

Layer chosen on transfer

In-domain CV picks layer 20; out-of-domain transfer picks 18. The deployed head reads 21. On the seven cells measured so far, a layer-18 head is +0.0289 macro against layer-20’s +0.0257 — small, but selected on the right metric for once.

The identification question

Two hypotheses are dead. The next candidates are properties of the pool rather than the domain — penalty is measurable without labels if we can estimate it, and it is the term that decides the sign.

The question that gates everything else. Until we can say in advance which cells selection helps, temperature has to be a single fixed choice rather than a per-cell one — and a fixed choice gives up most of the +0.0117 that per-cell tuning shows is there. That is the next result worth having.
