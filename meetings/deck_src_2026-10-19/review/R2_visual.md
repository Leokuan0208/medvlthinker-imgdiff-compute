- HIGH · s32 · The readout equation runs into the "why ranks" card: the end of "ties → arg max v_i" lies under the card's first line ("the 24 probes…") at x≈655–690, y≈450. · Put the tie-break on its own line under the equation (`\hat y=\arg\max_i r_i` / `\text{ties}\to\arg\max_i v_i`), or narrow the equation column to ≤560px with `overflow:visible` off. Also move the formula block up about 60px into the empty band at y≈300–370.
- HIGH · s43 · The legend (judge / MedGemma / lenient EM, y≈134) overlaps the chart title "variant − rank average (…)" (y≈121): the legend dots touch the title's descenders. The cause is `forest()` in deck_lib.py, which uses `top=34` when both a title and a legend are set, so the legend is drawn at y=top−14=20 on top of the title at y=14. · In `forest()`, set `top = 52 if (title and legend and ns>1) else (34 if … else 12)` and draw the legend at `y=top-12`. This fixes every forest that has both a title and a legend.
- HIGH · s18 · The majority-vote forest silently clips VQA-RAD's CI. The true lower bound is −0.0729 (NUMBERS_T07.json cell.vqa_rad_open.ci_sc_minus_greedy_judge), but the axis starts at −0.06 and `forest()` clamps with `max(lo,xmin)`. The interval therefore looks as if it ends at the axis. · Widen the axis to `forest(sc_rows, -0.08, 0.04, 0.02, …)` in deck_part1.py:571. Also make `forest()` draw a small arrowhead/chevron whenever `lo<xmin` or `hi>xmax`, so future clipping is visible.
- HIGH · s41 · The probe-size bar chart's y-axis starts at 0.64 and the slide does not say so. The linear bar (0.664) looks about a quarter of the height of 256 (0.694), but the real difference is 4%. Red fill makes it worse. · Use a dot plot (`lines()` with markers only, or a forest-style row) instead of bars. If the bars stay, start them at 0, or add "axis starts at 0.64" and a break mark. Also add the y-axis title "CV selection efficiency".
- HIGH · s54 · The Qwen table runs into the right-hand cards: the "SIGNIFICANT BENCHMARKS" header and the "6 / 8", "3 / 8" values touch the orange card bar at x≈658. The row labels wrap to 3 lines ("judge (MedVLThinker-32B)"). · Shorten the first column's labels to "judge", "lenient EM", "strict EM", "token-F1" (the judge's name is in the footnote). Rename the header to "sig." and add `padding-right:16px` / `gap:28px` between the columns.
- HIGH · s07 · Both charts are tiny (tick labels ≈8–9px, axis titles ≈9px) and float in white space. Neither y-axis starts at zero (distinct answers from 2, oracle@8 from 0.4), and the slide does not say so. · Render each chart at w≈330, h≈260 at native size (no CSS downscale), stacked vertically in the right column, with ticks ≥12px.
